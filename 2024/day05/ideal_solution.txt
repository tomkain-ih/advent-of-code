2. Defining the `order` Function
def order(nums): 
    return sorted(nums, key=lambda x: -sum(f"{x}|{y}" in rules for y in nums))
This function takes a list of numbers (`nums`) as input.
It sorts the numbers based on a custom key:
For each number `x` in `nums`, the key is calculated as:
The negative of the sum of occurrences of the string `"{x}|{y}"` in `rules`, where `y` iterates over all numbers in `nums`.
This means that numbers with more occurrences of `"{x}|{y}"` in `rules` will appear earlier in the sorted list.
The `-` sign ensures descending order (higher counts come first).
